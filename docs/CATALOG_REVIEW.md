# Catalogue review boundary

The v2 catalogue contains four neutral geometric identifiers: thumb-index,
thumb-middle, thumb-ring and thumb-middle-ring contact. Each record describes a
rule from the public specification, its fixed heuristic score, limitations and
Apache-2.0 provenance. A record is a learning cue, not a claim about a named
tradition, person, religion, health outcome or therapeutic effect.

Historical names such as Gyan, Chin, Shunya, Prithvi and Apana are migration
aliases only. They are not enabled in the public catalogue and are not inferred
by the geometric recognizer. A label pack may be added only with an explicit
reviewer identity, evidence reference, rights, language/tradition scope,
prohibited-claims record, version and withdrawal record. The loader validates
that record shape; it never turns an evidence-free record into approval.

Human community and accessibility review remains an external release decision.
No word filter, synthetic fixture or agent status flag can substitute for that
review. The public core stays useful offline while leaving that decision visible.
